Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also store the expense records you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. Basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, may be collected to help keep the Service secure and reliable.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries, category breakdowns, and other insights within the app. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our application database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers, such as hosting and font services, to operate the Service. These providers process data only as needed to deliver their service to us and are not authorized to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense records with third parties for marketing.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided within the app or on our website.
